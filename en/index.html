Our story

A story that begins with Manolo Rodríguez

More than 50 years ago, Manolo Rodríguez began his journey in the world of construction. Decades of work, effort and craft shaped a way of doing things based on integrity, keeping one's word and work done well.

Today, his sons Carlos Rodríguez and Sergio Rodríguez carry on that legacy under the name Rodríguez Brothers Construction, keeping the values of always while adapting to the current needs of each project.

Because for us, building isn't just about raising walls. It's about creating spaces where people live, work and build their future.
